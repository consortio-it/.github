# Consortio IT

**Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central partner in Southern Denmark.**

We implement, develop, support and advise on business systems built around Microsoft Dynamics NAV and Business Central, previously known as Navision. Alongside the ERP core we work with Dynamics C5, Microsoft 365, Power BI and Azure.

Based in Sønderborg, we are a team of 21 with more than 200 combined years of NAV, Business Central and C5 experience, working mainly with small and medium sized businesses across Southern Denmark.

## What we do

- Implementation and upgrade of Dynamics NAV and Business Central solutions
- New modules, customisations and extensions for existing NAV, Business Central and C5 installations
- Migration and licence conversion from Dynamics C5 to Business Central
- Hosting of Dynamics NAV and Business Central on Microsoft Azure
- Integration with the wider Microsoft stack, including Microsoft 365 and Power BI
- Reporting and business intelligence
- Advisory on licensing, system landscape, architecture and digitalisation
- Ongoing upgrade services that keep your Business Central solution current as new versions ship

## Accreditations

**Continia Premium Solution Partner**, covering Document Capture, Payment Management, Banking, Finance and Expense Management.
